# Dictation

The notes in this vault are the user's own thinking, so every sentence in one is a sentence they
said. Take their words down; do not improve them.

One file in `$GARDEN/garden/05 - Fleeting/`.

## Their words

Their sentences, in their register, in their order. Fix typos and obvious slips and nothing else.

Their phrasing is the hook that makes the note recognisable to them in a year, and a
half-expressed idea is data about where their understanding sits, so both survive intact. A note
you wrote out of your own research is your work sitting in their graph under their name.

## Shape

Bare prose. No frontmatter: 74 of the vault's 119 notes carry none, and the ones that do disagree
about their keys.

Filename follows the neighbours, so read the directory before naming.

- Dash-case for a tool or component: `traefik-ingress-controller.md`
- A prose title for an idea: `Go compilations versus Csharp and Java JIT.md`

## Anchors

Tags and wikilinks are interpretation, so offer them and add the ones the user takes.

- Tags are inline `#kebab-case` hashtags sitting in the sentence they qualify.
- Wikilinks carry the full vault path: `[[04 - Permanent/Python/ast — Abstract Syntax Trees]]`.
- Check every target on disk before writing it. The vault already carries broken links from
  `index.md`, and adding more makes the graph less trustworthy than no link at all.

**Done when:** every sentence in the file is one the user wrote or approved, and every wikilink
resolves.
